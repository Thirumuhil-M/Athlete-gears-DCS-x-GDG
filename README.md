# Athlete-gears-DCS-x-GDG
The Athlete Gear App is your all-in-one companion for performance-driven sportswear and equipment. Designed for runners, cyclists, gym enthusiasts, and outdoor adventurers, it helps you discover, compare, and shop the best gear tailored to your training needs.

Key Features:

🔍 Smart Gear Finder – Browse shoes, apparel, and accessories optimized for your sport.

📦 Seamless Shopping – Quick look previews, detailed product pages, and easy checkout.

🏅 Performance Insights – Learn how each product supports endurance, speed, and recovery.

📍 Store Details & Support – Access contact info, delivery policies, returns, and booking services.
