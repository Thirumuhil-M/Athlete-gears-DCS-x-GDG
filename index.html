/ About

Our Story

At Paceline, we run on coffee, pure passion, and the endless pursuit of the ultimate running high. Founded in Glasgow, we've built a curated space for athletes of all paces, packed with every essential for modern runners.

01 / Our Philosophy

We're all
about the run.

It all started back in 2015 when our founder, Rae, couldn't find a single specialist shop in the city that genuinely cared about the experience of running rather than merely shifting shoe boxes off high shelves.

Paceline was built to be different: an open, community-led haven where advice is honest, every brand has earned its spot through performance, and runners gathered to share miles before talking gear.

Ten years on, our obsession hasn't changed. If it's on our shelves, it's because we've personally run in it.

— Rae Sinclair & Jamie Roy, Co-Founders
02 / What Defines Us

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

Curated, not sold to us.

Every shoe and apparel silhouette we offer has passed rigorous testing on Scottish roads and trails. We say no to 80% of what brands pitch.

02

Community over transaction.

From our weekly 5K social shakeouts to marathon training long runs, we believe running is always better when shared with fellow obsessives.

03

Craft in every detail.

Whether analyzing your gait through 240fps slow-motion cameras or recommending sock thickness, detail is what prevents injury and unlocks PRs.
